# How to Measure Blood Pressure Accurately at Home

Home blood pressure monitoring (HBPM) is essential for effective hypertension management. According to the **American Heart Association (AHA)** and the **American Medical Association (AMA)**, following proper protocol is critical for meaningful readings.

## 30 Minutes Before Measuring

- **Avoid Caffeine, Alcohol, & Tobacco**: Do not smoke, drink coffee, or consume alcohol within 30 minutes prior to testing.
- **Empty Your Bladder**: A full bladder can increase systolic pressure by 10–15 mmHg.
- **Avoid Exercise**: Rest quietly for at least 5 minutes before taking the first measurement.

## Correct Measurement Posture

1. **Sit Comfortably**: Sit in a supportive chair with your back straight and supported.
2. **Feet Flat**: Keep both feet flat on the floor; do not cross your legs or ankles.
3. **Arm at Heart Level**: Rest your bare arm on a flat table or surface so that the cuff is at heart level.
4. **Cuff Placement**: Place the bottom edge of the cuff approximately 1 inch (2–3 cm) above the bend of your elbow. The cuff should fit snugly, allowing two fingertips to slip underneath.
5. **Remain Silent**: Do not talk, text, or read during the measurement. Even passive conversation can elevate readings by up to 10 mmHg.

## AHA Recommended Schedule

- Take **two readings**, 1 minute apart, in the morning before eating or taking medications.
- Take **two readings** in the evening before dinner or bed.
- Record readings for **7 consecutive days** prior to your healthcare provider appointment.
