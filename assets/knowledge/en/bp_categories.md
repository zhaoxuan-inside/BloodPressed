# Blood Pressure Categories: Where Do Your Numbers Stand?

This classification follows the landmark **2017 American College of Cardiology (ACC) / American Heart Association (AHA)** High Blood Pressure Guidelines for adults.

| Category | Systolic (mmHg) | | Diastolic (mmHg) | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **🟢 Normal** | Less than 120 | AND | Less than 80 | Maintain heart-healthy habits. |
| **🟡 Elevated** | 120 – 129 | AND | Less than 80 | Lifestyle interventions (DASH diet, exercise, weight loss). |
| **🟠 Stage 1 Hypertension** | 130 – 139 | OR | 80 – 89 | Lifestyle modifications ± medication based on ASCVD risk. |
| **🔴 Stage 2 Hypertension** | 140 or higher | OR | 90 or higher | Lifestyle changes + combination antihypertensive therapy. |
| **🚨 Hypertensive Crisis** | Higher than 180 | AND/OR | Higher than 120 | Emergency medical evaluation needed immediately. |

## Key Clinical Differences in U.S. Guidelines

In 2017, the AHA/ACC lowered the definition of high blood pressure to **130/80 mmHg** (previously 140/90 mmHg). This earlier intervention threshold is designed to encourage lifestyle interventions before organ damage accumulates.

### 🟡 Elevated (120–129 / <80)
People with elevated blood pressure are likely to develop hypertension unless lifestyle changes are adopted: reduce sodium, engage in regular aerobic activity, and minimize alcohol.

### 🟠 Stage 1 Hypertension (130–139 or 80–89)
If your 10-year cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) risk is under 10%, doctors typically begin with 3 to 6 months of lifestyle changes. If risk exceeds 10% or diabetes/kidney disease is present, medication is initiated promptly.

### 🔴 Stage 2 Hypertension (≥140 or ≥90)
Physicians typically prescribe two medications of different classes alongside lifestyle modifications to prevent stroke and cardiac events.

### 🟣 Hypotension (Low Blood Pressure)
Readings consistently below **90/60 mmHg** without dizziness, fainting, or blurred vision are generally harmless. If symptomatic or taking medication, inform your physician immediately.
